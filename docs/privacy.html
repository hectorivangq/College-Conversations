Privacy

Updated October 22, 2026

This page covers what this website collects when you visit.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, such as which pages are viewed and where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your device type, browser, approximate location and the pages you visit. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your roadmap checklist

When you tick items on the roadmap, the list of ticked items is saved in your own browser so it's there when you come back. It isn't sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Videos

A video loads from YouTube, in its privacy-enhanced mode, only when you press play. From then on, YouTube's privacy policy applies to that video.

Hosting and links

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs such as IP addresses for security. Links in the library go to outside sites, and their own privacy policies apply there.

Your choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on, or block or clear cookies in your browser.
